DigitalBoda Account Deletion

Request to delete your account and associated data

About DigitalBoda

DigitalBoda is a comprehensive platform for motorcycle (boda boda) riders, providing digital literacy training, onboarding services, and professional development opportunities.

How to Request Account Deletion

1
Fill out the form below

Provide your phone number (the one you used to register) and optionally tell us why you want to delete your account.

2
Submit your request

Click the "Request Account Deletion" button to submit your request to our system.

3
Confirmation

You will receive a confirmation message once your request is received.

4
Processing

Your account and associated data will be deleted within 30 days of your request.

Data Deletion Policy

Data That Will Be Deleted:

Data Type Description
Personal Information Name, phone number, email address
Profile Data Profile photos, personal details, preferences
Training Progress Digital literacy course progress and completion records
Application Data Onboarding application and verification documents
Authentication Data Login credentials and access tokens

Data That May Be Retained:

Data Type Retention Period Reason
Transaction Records 7 years Legal and regulatory compliance
Anonymized Analytics Indefinite Service improvement (no personal identifiers)
Audit Logs 90 days Security and fraud prevention
Important Notice

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your training progress, certificates, and achievements will be lost. Please ensure you have downloaded any important data before proceeding.
